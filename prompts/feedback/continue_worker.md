Your `finished.md` file should include your experimental setup, methodology, implementation details, results, interpretation of results, and next steps. Be sure to include all relevant filepaths.

You've already made some progress, so you should continue your project from there. Reference your most recent progress at `memories/finished_{finished_timestamp}.md` and feedback on your progress at `memories/feedback_{feedback_timestamp}.md`. Earlier progress logs and feedback are also available in `memories/`.
